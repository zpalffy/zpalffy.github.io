Privacy & About

Everything on this site runs entirely in your browser. When you paste CSV data, it is parsed locally using Papa Parse — nothing is ever sent to a server, stored in a database, or transmitted anywhere.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies.

Your data is yours and never leaves your device.
