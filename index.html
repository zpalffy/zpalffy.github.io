Browser Tools & Games

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing leaves your device.

A small collection of tools I kept wishing existed without the ads, the sign-up walls, or the "paste your sensitive data into our server" energy. Everything here is client-side — when you paste a JWT or generate a password, it never touches a server. Built with vanilla JS and minimal dependencies. No frameworks, no tracking, no nonsense.

Privacy & About

Everything on this site runs entirely in your browser. When you paste text, it is processed locally by JavaScript — nothing is ever sent to a server, stored in a database, or transmitted anywhere.

No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies.

Your data is yours and never leaves your device.
