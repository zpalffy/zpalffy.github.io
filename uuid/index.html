        Privacy & About
      

      
UUIDs are generated locally in your browser using crypto.randomUUID() — the same Web Crypto API used by operating systems for secure random number generation. Nothing is ever sent to a server, stored in a database, or transmitted anywhere.

      
No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies.

      
Your data is yours and never leaves your device.
