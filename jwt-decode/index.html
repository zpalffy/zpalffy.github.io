        Privacy & About
      

      
Everything on this site runs entirely in your browser. When you paste a JWT token, it is decoded locally using JavaScript — nothing is ever sent to a server, stored in a database, or transmitted anywhere.

      
No accounts. No analytics. No tracking. No cookies.

      
Your data is yours and never leaves your device.
