About the Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique breaks work into focused intervals (traditionally 25 minutes) separated by short breaks. After four intervals, take a longer break.

Space bar to start/pause. Sound and browser notifications fire when each phase ends — notifications require a one-time permission prompt.

Everything runs in your browser. No accounts. No analytics. No tracking.
